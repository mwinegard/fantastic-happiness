Quick Help — Specialty Cards
🃏 Draw Stacks (+2 / +4)
Draw penalties stack. If a draw stack is on you, you must either:
  • Stack another Draw 2 or Wild Draw 4, or
  • Play RELAX (cancels the stack), or
  • Draw the full stack (then the stack clears and your turn ends).
🧘 RELAX (Wild)
Can be played as a normal Wild on your turn (choose a color), or played out of turn to cancel a draw stack.
🌈 Rainbow (Wild)
Requires you to have one of each color: Red + Yellow + Green + Blue. You discard one of each under the pile, then choose a color.
👑 The Boss (Wild)
Picks “The Boss” by scoreboard (points/wins). If Lori is playing, Lori is always The Boss.
Every player gifts 1 random card to the Boss, then the player who played it chooses a color.
🧳 Pack Your Bags (Wild)
Randomly swaps seats — and you take the hand of the seat you land in (hands move with seats).
Cannot be played during a draw stack. (Stack must be resolved first.)
Then choose a color.
🛒 Shopping (Yellow-only)
Only playable when the active color is Yellow.
You pick a target, secretly give 2 of your cards, then secretly take 1 from them.
🤝 Pinky Promise (Yellow-only)
Only playable when the active color is Yellow.
Always allowed. You choose another player; both hands combine, shuffle, and split again.
♻️ Recycle (Green-only)
Only playable when the active color is Green.
Everyone’s hands combine, shuffle, and are re-dealt. The game will force draws as needed so every player receives at least 1 card.
😊 Happy Mode (Green)
Turns on “Happy Mode.” While active:
If someone types rude in chat, the previous chatter draws 1 card.
🌙 To The Moon (Blue-only)
Only playable when the active color is Blue.
A random other player gets +1 card.
👀 Blue Look (Blue-only)
Only playable when the active color is Blue.
You look at the top cards of the deck and reorder them (1 = next draw).
📄 NOC Notice (Red-only)
Only playable when the active color is Red.
First time: picks a random target (including yourself) who draws 3.
Later uses the same target again for another +3.
🧢 IT (Red-only)
Only playable when the active color is Red.
With 3+ players: moves 1 random card from the previous player to the next player (“we all float…”).
With 2 players: behaves like Draw 2.
